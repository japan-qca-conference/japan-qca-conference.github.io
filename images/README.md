# Images

Only images approved by the organizers for public use belong in this directory.

- Confirm publication rights for logos, venue photos, maps, and flyers
- Do not add identifiable participant photos or internal documents
- Optimize images for the web and provide meaningful alternative text
- Preserve source and rights information where attribution is required

The favicon files (`favicon.ico`, `favicon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, and the root `favicon.ico`) were generated from the Japan QCA Conference logo (circular badge) supplied for the website. The white background of the logo was made transparent for the favicon files; the Apple touch icon is flattened onto white.

The two tracked `hero-*.png` files are AI-generated illustrations, not documentary photographs. They were selected by the organizers for the Home-page rotation and retain C2PA provenance metadata.
