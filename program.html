Final programme for the Japan QCA Conference 2026

Final programme

The Japan QCA Conference will take place on 30–31 October 2026 at Komatsu Hall, Building 121, B1F, Waseda University, Tokyo. All times below are in Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9).

View the final programme (PDF) Download the PDF

The PDF includes the complete programme in English and Japanese, with presentation titles, speakers, discussants, and session assignments.

Participation and registration

Advance registration is required. Please register using the conference participation form and indicate whether you will attend the dinner reception on 30 October.

Register for the conference via Google Forms ↗

Keynote speeches and Paper Development Workshops are in person. Roundtables and Expert Workshops are hybrid, combining in-person and online participation.

Time (JST) Session

12:00–13:00 Lunch
13:00–14:30 Paper Development Workshop I — Sessions I and II
14:45–15:45 Roundtable: QCA as an Approach (hybrid) — Moderator & Facilitator: Yuko Kasuya; Panelists: Peer Fiss, Carsten Schneider, and Srikrishna Ayyangar (online)

18:00 Reception
